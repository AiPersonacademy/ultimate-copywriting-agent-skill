Download the ZIP → extract into `~/.agents/skills/ultimate-copywriting/` → done.

## 🧬 What This Is

This is an **AI Agent Skill** — a structured knowledge module that AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Amp, Antigravity, Cursor, etc.) can read and apply when drafting personalized copy.

It fuses three of the most influential copywriting books ever written into one unified, agent-readable system:

| Book | Author | What It Brings |
|:-----|:-------|:---------------|
| **The Adweek Copywriting Handbook** | Joseph Sugarman | Slippery Slide, Copy Sequence, Seven Steps, 30+ real ad teardowns |
| **CA$HVERTISING** | Drew Eric Whitman | Life-Force 8, 17 Psychology Principles, 41 Ad-Agency Secrets, 46-Point Killer Checklist |
| **Scientific Advertising** | Claude C. Hopkins | Reason-Why Copy, Keyed Advertising, Test-and-Measure, Salesmanship in Print |

> **730 pages → 20 thematic chapters → 25 agent-ready files → one skill folder.**

## 🔥 Frameworks Inside

### Core Psychological Frameworks
- **🧬 Life-Force 8 (LF8)** — 8 biologically hardwired desires that drive all purchasing
- **🎢 The Slippery Slide** — Every element pulls the reader to the next line
- **🎬 Mental Movies** — Sensory language that makes readers experience the product in their minds
- **🪞 Ego-Morphing** — Making readers mentally become the person who already owns the product
- **🧠 ELM (Elaboration Likelihood Model)** — Central vs peripheral persuasion routes
- **💊 Cure vs. Prevention** — Why solutions outsell prevention 10:1

### Copywriting Systems
- **📋 Seven Steps to Great Copy** (Sugarman)
- **📐 The Copy Sequence** — Headline → Subhead → Body → Proof → CTA
- **✂️ The Cleverectomy Principle** — Why clarity crushes cleverness
- **🏗️ The Credibility Stack** — Layering proof for maximum trust
- **📖 Story Selling Framework** — Narrative structures that bypass objections
- **🔬 Hopkins Test-and-Measure** — Scientific A/B testing from 1923

### Tactical Checklists
- **22 Psychologically Potent Headline Starters**
- **22 Response Superchargers**
- **9 Ways to Convey Value**
- **13 Ways to Make Buying Easy**
- **46-Point Killer Ad Checklist**
