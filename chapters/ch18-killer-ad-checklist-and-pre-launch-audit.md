</Chapter 18: Killer Ad Checklist and Pre-Launch Audit>
> Fuses: Sugarman Ch 23, Whitman 46-Point Checklist & Boosters

## Core Idea
Before any piece of copy goes live, it must pass a rigorous, systematic audit to ensure it contains all necessary psychological triggers, removes all friction, and maximizes the probability of response.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The 7-Step Copy Process**: (Sugarman) The complete workflow for generating winning copy.
  - When to use: From project kickoff to final draft.
  - How: 1. Become an expert, 2. Know your prospect, 3. Craft headlines, 4. Write the copy freely, 5. Edit for clarity, 6. Incubate, 7. Final polish.

- **The 46-Point Killer Ad Checklist**: (Whitman) A comprehensive diagnostic tool to evaluate your ad's effectiveness.
  - When to use: During the final editing phase (Step 5/7).
  - How: Check every element against the list (e.g., Is the headline a stopper? Is the offer clear? Is there a call to action? Is there risk reversal?).

- **Response Superchargers & Value Boosters**: (Whitman) Tactical additions to significantly increase conversion rates.
  - When to use: When the core copy is solid but needs a higher conversion rate.
  - How: Add urgency, scarcity, free bonuses, extended guarantees, premium packaging, or fast-action rewards.

## Key Concepts
- **Incubation**: Stepping away from the draft for 24-48 hours to let the subconscious process it and return with a fresh perspective.
- **The Friction Audit**: Systematically identifying and removing any word, phrase, or step that makes it harder for the prospect to buy.
- **Value Stacking**: Adding bonuses and premiums to make the offer seem overwhelmingly valuable compared to the price.
- **The Call to Action (CTA)**: The exact, explicit instructions on what the reader must do right now to get the product.

## Mental Models
- **Think of the Checklist as a Pre-Flight Inspection**: A pilot doesn't guess if the flaps work; they check. Never launch an ad based on "feel"—run the audit.
- **Use "The Scales" when evaluating the offer**: The perceived value (product + bonuses + risk reversal) must heavily outweigh the perceived cost (price + hassle).

## Anti-patterns
- **Skipping Incubation**: Publishing the first draft immediately while you are still "in love" with your own words, blinding you to glaring flaws.
- **Assuming the Obvious**: Failing to explicitly tell the reader *how* to buy because you assume they know. (e.g., Missing the CTA or contact info).

## Worked Example
*Applying Value Boosters to a basic offer*
- **Basic Offer**: "Buy our copywriting course for $199."
- **Supercharged Offer**: "Get the complete Copywriting Masterclass for just $199. Plus, if you order by midnight tonight, you'll get 3 free bonuses: The swipe file of 100 winning ads (Value: $50), our 30-day email templates (Value: $99), and a 1-on-1 copy critique (Value: $150). Try it risk-free for 60 days. If it doesn't double your conversions, we'll refund every penny and you keep the bonuses. Click the big orange button below to start."

## Key Takeaways
1. Great copy is built through a structured process, not sudden flashes of pure inspiration.
2. Always write freely first, then edit ruthlessly later.
3. Walk away from your copy (incubate) before the final edit.
4. Run every ad through a strict checklist to ensure no fundamental elements (headline, proof, CTA, guarantee) are missing.
5. Supercharge your response rates by stacking relevant, high-value bonuses and removing all perceived risk.
