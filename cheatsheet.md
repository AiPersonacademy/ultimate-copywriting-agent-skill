# Cheatsheet — Ultimate Copywriting Skill

## Decision: Which LF8 Drive to Lead With?

| If the product... | Lead with LF8 | Hook angle |
|---|---|---|
| Solves a health/safety problem | #1 Survival, #3 Freedom from fear | "Stop [threat] before it stops you" |
| Is food/beverage/consumable | #2 Enjoyment of food | Sensory language, mental movies |
| Removes anxiety or risk | #3 Freedom from fear/pain | Fear-resolution arc |
| Makes the buyer more attractive | #4 Sexual companionship | Ego-morphing, aspirational identity |
| Improves daily comfort/convenience | #5 Comfortable living | Before/after contrast |
| Confers status or competitive edge | #6 Superiority/winning | Social proof, exclusivity |
| Protects family/children | #7 Care of loved ones | Parental instinct trigger |
| Helps user fit in or be admired | #8 Social approval | Bandwagon, testimonials |

## Decision: Emotion-First or Logic-First?

| Reader state | Route | What to lead with |
|---|---|---|
| High involvement (researching, comparing) | Central (ELM) | Facts, specs, comparisons, reason-why |
| Low involvement (browsing, scrolling) | Peripheral (ELM) | Authority cues, social proof, visual appeal |
| Already aware of the problem | Cure framing | "Here's how to fix [their pain]" |
| Not aware of the problem | Fear → resolution | "You may not know this, but [threat]…" |

## Decision: Long Copy or Short Copy?

| Condition | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| High price (>\$50) | Long copy | Must overcome price resistance with proof |
| Complex product | Long copy | Must educate before selling |
| Direct response / mail order | Long copy | Must close the sale in the copy itself |
| Established brand + simple product | Short copy | Awareness is the goal, not the close |
| Social media / display ads | Short (headline + CTA) | Attention window is 1-3 seconds |
| Email subject + preview | Ultra-short (6-10 words) | One job: get the open |

## Headline Decision Tree

```
Is the biggest benefit clear in the headline?
├── NO → Rewrite. Biggest benefit first, always.
└── YES
    ├── Is it specific? (numbers, names, timeframes)
    │   ├── NO → Add specificity. "Saves 33.7%" beats "saves money."
    │   └── YES
    │       ├── Does it select the right audience?
    │       │   ├── NO → Add audience qualifier. "For [type of person]…"
    │       │   └── YES → Test it. Write 24 more variations.
    └── Is it trying to be clever? → Cleverectomy. Remove the pun.
```

## The Copy Sequence (Mandatory Order)

1. **Headline** → State biggest benefit, select audience, create curiosity
2. **Subheadline** → Expand the promise, reinforce benefit
3. **Image + Caption** → Show the result; caption is the 2nd-most-read element
4. **Opening sentence** → Short, punchy, personal. Start the slippery slide
5. **Problem agitation** → Name the pain, amplify with specifics
6. **Solution reveal** → Introduce the product as the cure
7. **Benefits stack** → Bullet each benefit with PVAs (positive visual adjectives)
8. **Proof stack** → Testimonials + specifics + authority + social proof
9. **Value framing** → Anchor high, amortize, compare
10. **Risk reversal** → Guarantee, trial, money-back
11. **Urgency/scarcity** → Deadline or limited quantity
12. **CTA** → Explicit, single action. "Order Now" — not "Learn More"

## Pricing Quick-Reference

| Goal | Tactic | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Signal value/deal | Odd-number endings | $19.97, $49.99 |
| Signal premium quality | Round numbers | $50, $1,000 |
| Reduce sticker shock | Amortize to daily cost | "Just $1.25/day" |
| Boost perceived value | Anchor then discount | "Worth $2,000 — yours for $497" |
| Increase conversions 15%+ | Offer installments | "3 easy payments of $33" |

## Readability Thresholds

| Target audience | Fog Index grade level | Words per sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Mass market | 8th-10th grade | 11-17 words |
| Business professionals | 10th-12th grade | 15-20 words |
| Technical/academic | 12th+ grade | 20-25 words |

**Rule of thumb**: If a 12-year-old can't understand your ad, rewrite it.

## Red Flags (Tells of Bad Copy)

- ❌ Headline is clever but doesn't state a benefit
- ❌ First sentence talks about the company, not the reader
- ❌ No "you" in the first 50 words
- ❌ Reverse type (white on black) for body copy
- ❌ More than 3 typefaces
- ❌ No testimonials anywhere
- ❌ No guarantee or risk reversal
- ❌ No deadline or urgency mechanism
- ❌ CTA says "Learn More" instead of a specific action
- ❌ Price shown without value framing
