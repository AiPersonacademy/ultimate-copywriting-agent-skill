# Headline Formula Templates

Use these fill-in-the-blank formulas. Each is backed by decades of tested results from Caples, Ogilvy, Hopkins, Whitman, and Sugarman.

---

## The 22 Psychologically Potent Headline Starters (Whitman)

1. **FREE!** [benefit or item]
2. **NEW** — [product/feature that solves problem]
3. **AT LAST** — [solution to longstanding frustration]
4. **This is the** [superlative] [product category] **ever** [developed/created]
5. **WARNING!** [consequence of not acting]
6. **Just Released** — [what it is and why it matters]
7. **Now You Can** [desirable action] **and** [additional benefit]
8. **If You** [condition matching target audience], **You Can** [benefit]
9. **Advice to** [target audience] **Who Want to** [desired outcome]
10. **The Truth About** [topic they're curious/skeptical about]
11. **If You're** [pain point], **Here's** [solution]
12. **Here Is** [category-breaking claim]
13. **[Famous person]** [does surprising thing related to product]
14. **Give Me** [small commitment] **and I'll** [big promise]
15. **[Specific number]** [surprising facts/ways/reasons] **to** [benefit]
16. **WANTED** — [target audience] **for** [desirable opportunity]
17. **This** [product] [accomplishes result] **in** [specific timeframe]
18. **[Achieve desired outcome]** — **or Your Money Back**
19. **You** [strong action verb] [familiar situation]... **and then** [unexpected twist]
20. **Do You** [provocative question about common belief]?
21. **How** [seemingly simple action] **Made Me** [impressive result]
22. **Are You** [embarrassing/undesirable situation]? **Here's How to** [fix it]

## Power Headline Formulas

### The "How To" Formula
> How to [achieve desired outcome] without [common objection/obstacle]

**Example**: "How to Double Your Sales Without Spending a Dime on Ads"

### The Specificity Formula
> [Exact number] [thing] that [produce specific result] in [timeframe]

**Example**: "7 Email Subject Lines That Generated $43,000 in 72 Hours"

### The Curiosity-Gap Formula
> [Unexpected claim] — [qualifier that makes them need to read more]

**Example**: "Why Bad Grammar Sells More Than Perfect Copy — And When to Use It"

### The Authority Formula
> [Expert/source] reveals [insider knowledge] about [topic they care about]

**Example**: "What $100M-Revenue Companies Know About Pricing That You Don't"

### The Problem-Agitation Formula
> [Relatable frustration]? Here's [what actually works]

**Example**: "Tired of Ads That Cost a Fortune and Produce Nothing? Here's What Changed Everything for 2,000+ Small Businesses"

## Headline Quality Checklist
Before using any headline, verify:
- [ ] States biggest benefit?
- [ ] Is specific (numbers, names, timeframes)?
- [ ] Selects the right audience?
- [ ] Creates enough curiosity to pull into body copy?
- [ ] Free from cleverness/puns? (Cleverectomy passed?)
- [ ] Under 17 words for print; under 10 for email subjects?
