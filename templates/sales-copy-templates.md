# Sales Copy Templates

Ready-to-use structural templates for the most common copy formats. Fill in the [brackets].

---

## Template 1: Sales Email Sequence (3-Email Mini-Funnel)

### Email 1 — The Problem (Day 0)
```
Subject: [Specific pain point as a question]?

[First name],

[1-sentence vivid description of the pain they're experiencing right now.]

[2-3 sentences amplifying the consequences of NOT solving it — use LF8 #3 (freedom from fear/pain).]

I spent [timeframe] figuring out [topic], and I discovered [surprising insight].

[Tomorrow/In my next email], I'll share [specific thing you'll reveal].

[Signoff],
[Name]

P.S. [Restate the problem + hint at the solution]
```

### Email 2 — The Story (Day 1-2)
```
Subject: How [person/I] went from [bad state] to [good state]

[First name],

[Yesterday/Last time] I told you about [problem].

Here's what happened to [me/client name]:

[3-5 sentence story following the Story Selling Framework:
 - Relatable starting point
 - Specific obstacle
 - The discovery/turning point
 - Measurable result]

The key was [one-sentence principle/method].

Want to know exactly how? [Link to sales page / reply for details]

[Signoff],
[Name]

P.S. [Urgency element — deadline, limited spots, price increase]
```

### Email 3 — The Offer (Day 2-3)
```
Subject: [Time-sensitive] — [benefit] for [audience]

[First name],

Quick recap: [1 sentence restating the transformation]

Here's everything you get with [product name]:

✅ [Benefit 1 — framed as outcome, not feature]
✅ [Benefit 2]
✅ [Benefit 3]
✅ [Bonus 1 — "Plus, you also get..."]
✅ [Bonus 2]

Total value: [$X,XXX]
Your price today: [$XXX]

🔒 [Guarantee — "Try it for [30/60/90] days. If [specific condition], I'll refund every penny."]

⏰ [Urgency — "This offer expires [date/time]" OR "Only [N] spots remaining"]

[CTA Button: Specific action — "Get [Product] Now" / "Start Your Free Trial"]

[Signoff],
[Name]

P.S. [Final benefit + urgency restatement]
```

## Template 2: Landing Page Structure

```
[ABOVE THE FOLD]
Headline: [Biggest benefit — use headline formula]
Subheadline: [Expand the promise + select audience]
Hero image: [Show the result, not the product]
CTA button: [Specific action verb + benefit]

[PROBLEM SECTION]
"Do you ever feel like [relatable frustration]?"
• [Pain point 1 — specific, visceral]
• [Pain point 2]
• [Pain point 3]
"You're not alone. [Social proof stat]."

[SOLUTION SECTION]
"Introducing [Product Name]"
[1 paragraph: what it is + how it works, framed as the cure]
[3-5 benefit bullets with PVAs — each starts with an action verb]

[PROOF SECTION]
"Don't just take our word for it"
[Testimonial 1 — name, photo, specific result]
[Testimonial 2]
[Testimonial 3]
[Authority badges / press mentions / certifications]

[VALUE SECTION]
"Here's Everything You Get:"
[Feature → Benefit list with value anchoring]
Total value: $[high anchor]
Your investment: $[actual price — use psychological pricing]

[RISK REVERSAL]
"Our [X]-Day [Name] Guarantee"
[Specific guarantee language — longer than competitors']

[URGENCY]
[Deadline OR scarcity OR bonus expiration]

[FINAL CTA]
[Same CTA button as above]
[Short reassurance line: "Secure checkout. Cancel anytime."]
```

## Template 3: Short-Form Ad (Social / Display / Print)

```
[HEADLINE — 5-10 words max, biggest benefit, specific]
[IMAGE — Show result or person experiencing result]

[BODY — 2-4 sentences max:]
[Sentence 1: Problem acknowledgment — "you" within first 3 words]
[Sentence 2: Solution + key differentiator]
[Sentence 3: Proof point — specific number or testimonial snippet]

[CTA: Single, clear action]
[OFFER: What they get + urgency element]
```

## Template 4: Direct Mail / Sales Letter

```
[Date]

Dear [First name / "Friend"],

[Opening hook — story, question, or bold claim. Must start the Slippery Slide.]

[Problem agitation — 2-3 paragraphs. Name the pain, amplify consequences.]

[The reveal — "That's why I created [product]"]

[Benefits — bulleted list, 7-12 items, each framed as outcomes]

[Credibility stack — who you are, why you're qualified, social proof]

[The offer — everything they get, value anchoring]

[Price reveal + value framing — amortize, compare, anchor]

[Guarantee — bold, specific, longer than competitors']

[Urgency — deadline, limited supply, or bonus expiration]

[Call to action — specific instructions: "Call now," "Visit," "Mail the enclosed card"]

Sincerely,
[Signature]
[Name + title/credentials]

P.S. [Restate the single biggest benefit + urgency. The P.S. is read first by 79% of readers.]

P.P.S. [Restate the guarantee]
```
